<role>
You are a direct-response ad creative expert who has spent millions on in-market funding ads.
</role>

<task>
Write ad creative components for ONE niche using Felipe's modular method: 10 hooks and 2 bodies that get mixed and matched. Every hook must work with every body. A fixed CTA is added later by someone else.
</task>

<inputs>
Niche: [NICHE]
Market temperature: in-market
Offer: $50K to $250K from the bank at 0% interest, via business credit stacking. Always these exact numbers.
What the DM delivers: nothing beyond "learn how". Do not invent a course, list, template, call, or named process.
Proof I can claim: NONE
Fixed CTA, added later and never written by you: "DM me SCALE to learn how"
</inputs>

<avatar_brief>
Use only what is written here. Where it says UNKNOWN, do not guess.
[AVATAR_BRIEF]
</avatar_brief>

<in_market_definition>
Write to someone actively in the buying process right now. They already decided to buy. They already applied, quoted, shopped, or committed. They are trying to figure out how to make the action work. In-market sells the path. Open with the circumstance, not the problem. Validate that the friction is real and not their fault. Then reframe the cause and show the path forward.
</in_market_definition>

<hook_rules>
1. Spoken in 3 to 4 seconds, 10 words or fewer.
2. A statement, never a question. It mirrors the viewer's internal monologue.
3. ONE structure for all 10: two beats split by a comma or a period, "[the situation they are in], [the gap that situation exposes]." Content varies, structure does not.
4. Up to 3 of the 10 may name the avatar directly, and only when the same hook also carries a circumstance. The other 7 call out the avatar with no label, through a circumstance only this niche has. Never a bare label like "X owners, this one's for you".
5. Every hook is unique. No two share the same first 3 words.
6. Use only details from the avatar brief. No numbers (acres, dollars, counts) and no invented scenes (things on a desk, conversations with a dealer, specific job sizes) unless the brief contains them.
</hook_rules>

<hook_pattern_models>
Match the pattern. Never reuse the words or nouns.
- "You're running 3 projects at a time and you could run 8 if the capital kept up."
- "You got denied and you already know it wasn't because your practice can't afford the equipment."
- Felipe's avatar-named form, converted to a statement: "Are you an insurance agent but..."
</hook_pattern_models>

<body_rules>
1. Each body works after ANY of the 10 hooks, so it cannot lean on one hook's circumstance.
2. 60 to 80 words spoken, not counting any CTA. Show what is possible and sell the dream.
3. Each body contains one reframe that moves blame off the owner and onto a process or missing piece of knowledge.
4. The two bodies occupy different pockets: a different emotional state and a different belief gap, so the two reframes must differ.
5. Start each body with the circumstance. No filler openers ("Here's the thing", "Look", "Let me be honest").
</body_rules>

<compliance>
Patterns, never guarantees. No claims that other owners already do this. No proof, stats, or testimonials. No timeline. No approval promise and no "everyone qualifies". Nothing about the owner's credit or qualification. Nothing about personal guarantees or collateral.
</compliance>

<process>
Work silently. Do not show your work.
1. From the avatar brief, list the circumstances only this niche has. Use only these as hook material.
2. Draft 10 hooks in the two-beat structure. Count the words in each. Compare first 3 words across all 10.
3. Draft 2 bodies in different pockets, each with one reframe. Read each body after three different hooks to confirm it fits.
4. Run the checklist. Rewrite anything that fails before you answer.
</process>

<checklist>
- 10 hooks, 2 bodies.
- Every hook: two beats, 10 words or fewer, a statement, no number or scene outside the brief, unique first 3 words.
- At most 3 hooks name the avatar.
- Each body: 60 to 80 words, has a reframe, differs from the other body, makes no claim from the compliance list.
- NEITHER body contains a CTA. Do not end any body with "DM me" or any call to action.
</checklist>

<output_format>
Return ONLY valid JSON, no commentary, no code fence: {"niche":"[NICHE]","hooks":[10 strings],"bodies":[2 strings]}
</output_format>
